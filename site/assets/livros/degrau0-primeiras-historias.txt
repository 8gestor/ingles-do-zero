First Stories

1. The Fox and the Crow

A crow has a piece of cheese. She sits in a tree. She is happy.

A fox is under the tree. He is hungry. He sees the cheese. He wants the cheese.

"Hello, Crow," says the fox. "You are very beautiful today. I hear you have a beautiful voice. Please, sing for me."

The crow is very proud. She opens her mouth to sing. The cheese falls down.

The fox takes the cheese. "Thank you," he says. "Now I know something. Be careful with people who say nice things only to get something from you."

2. The Tortoise and the Hare

A hare and a tortoise are friends. The hare is very fast. The tortoise is very slow.

"I am the fastest animal in the forest," says the hare. "Nobody can win a race against me."

"I can," says the tortoise. "Let's race."

All the animals come to watch. The race starts. The hare runs very fast. Soon he cannot see the tortoise. He stops under a tree. "I have a lot of time," he thinks. "I will sleep a little."

The tortoise does not stop. He walks and walks. Step by step, he goes past the sleeping hare.

The hare wakes up. He runs and runs, but it is too late. The tortoise is at the end of the race.

Slow and steady wins the race.

3. The Boy Who Cried Wolf

A boy looks after the sheep of his village. Every day he sits on a hill. It is a boring job.

One day he has an idea. "Wolf! Wolf!" he shouts. "A wolf is eating the sheep!"

The people of the village run to the hill. They see no wolf. The boy laughs. The people are angry.

The next day he does it again. "Wolf! Wolf!" The people run to the hill again. Again there is no wolf. The boy laughs again.

Then, one evening, a real wolf comes. "Wolf! Wolf!" shouts the boy. "Please, help me!"

But nobody comes. The people think, "He is lying again." The wolf eats the sheep.

Nobody believes a person who lies, even when that person tells the truth.

4. The Lion and the Mouse

A lion is sleeping in the sun. A little mouse runs over his nose. The lion wakes up. He is very angry. He holds the mouse in his big paw.

"Please, let me go," says the mouse. "One day I will help you."

The lion laughs. "You? You are too small to help me." But he lets the mouse go.

A few days later, hunters catch the lion in a net. He pulls and pulls, but he cannot get out. He roars.

The mouse hears him. She runs to the lion. She bites the net with her small teeth, again and again. Soon the net is open.

"Now you are free," says the mouse. "You laughed at me, but now you see: even a small friend can help a big friend."

5. The Ant and the Grasshopper

It is summer. The sun is warm. A grasshopper sings and plays all day.

An ant works hard. She carries food to her house, again and again.

"Why do you work?" asks the grasshopper. "Come and play with me!"

"I am getting food for the winter," says the ant. "You should do the same."

"Winter is far away," says the grasshopper. And he goes on singing.

Then winter comes. It is cold. There is snow everywhere. The grasshopper has no food and no house. He goes to the ant. "Please, give me some food."

The ant looks at him. "In summer you sang. Now you can think about it." But the ant is kind, and she gives him a little food.

The grasshopper learns something. Next summer, he will work too.

6. The Dog and His Shadow

A dog has a big piece of meat. He is walking home with it in his mouth.

He crosses a small bridge over a river. He looks down. In the water, he sees another dog. That dog has a big piece of meat too.

"His meat is bigger than mine," thinks the dog. "I want it."

He opens his mouth to take the other meat. His own meat falls into the river. The water takes it away.

The dog looks down again. The other dog is gone. It was only his shadow.

Now he has nothing. If you want everything, you can lose what you have.
